Idempotency lab

A repeated request (double click, retry after a network cut) or a webhook delivered twice should not charge or notify twice. Turn the protection off to see what happens without it, then turn it back on to compare.

  1. Double submit when creating the payment. Pick a product above and press : two identical requests at once.
  2. Duplicate webhook. In the orders table, "×2" makes the gateway deliver the same payment notice twice. "×1" repeats a notice that was already sent.
  3. Failure halfway. Raise the random failure and notify a payment: see how the order, the charge and the SMS end up with and without protection.
  4. Payment charged but not recorded. Pay with a test card while the webhook is off (or failing) and press .
